1 · The idea

The community in one page

One place for the specialists who work with Special Minds and the families they support, in place of scattered WhatsApp groups. It is our own app, in English and Arabic, and Special Minds decides who joins and who can post where.

A spaceOne community for one group in one country: Specialists · UAE, Families · UAE, Specialists · India
ChannelsThe rooms inside a space: news, chat, questions, opportunities, events
PostsMessages, topics, opportunities and events
RepliesThreads, comments, reactions, votes and "I'm interested"

2 · Getting in

Joining

Specialists join with an invitation from Special Minds. Families ask to join, and a Special Minds admin approves them.

Specialists: an invitation

Special Minds sends you a link, or shows a QR code at a training day. Your own invite may already hold your role, city and languages from the Talent portal. Open the link, confirm your email or mobile number with a code (section 3), and you're in.

Families: ask to join

  1. Open community.specialminds.app/join, or tap Ask to join on the sign-in page.
  2. Choose Families · UAE.
  3. Confirm your email or mobile number with the 6-digit code you receive.
  4. Answer two short questions, such as your child's age and how you heard about Special Minds.
  5. Wait for an admin to approve you, usually within a day. You're told as soon as you're in.
Your detailsAfter you join, add your name, the name others see, your city and your languages in My account. Specialists also add their roles, skills and availability (section 12).
3 · Every day

Signing in

There is no password to remember. You sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email, or by WhatsApp to your mobile number.

  1. Open community.specialminds.app.
  2. Type your email, or your mobile number with its country code, like +971 50 123 4567. Press Send my code.
  3. Type the 6-digit code from your email or WhatsApp. It works for 10 minutes.

You stay signed in on that phone or computer for 30 days. Use the same email or number every time: it's how the community knows you.

Never share your codeSpecial Minds will never ask you for it. If you ask for more than six codes in an hour, wait an hour and try again.
ArabicThe العربية button at the top of the sign-in page switches the whole community to Arabic.
4 · Around the screen

Finding your way

On a computer the community has three parts: your spaces on the far left, the channels of the space you're in, and the channel itself.

1Your spaces. One icon each. Below them: Messages, Search and your notifications.
2The space you're in. Its name and what it's for.
3Channels. Bold means something new; a number counts it. A padlock is a private channel.
4Messages. Your private conversations (section 10).
5The channel. Click its name for what it's for, and to leave it.
6Members and alerts. How many are in it, and what you're told about (section 11).
7Write here. Press Enter to send, Shift and Enter for a new line.

More channels

Browse channels, at the bottom of the channel list, shows every channel in the space that you can join, such as groups by city, diagnosis or language. Click Join or Leave. Some channels are private: Special Minds adds the right people to them.

5 · Talking

Talking in a channel

Write in the box at the bottom of a channel and press Enter. Everyone in the channel sees it straight away.

Mentions

Type @ and pick a name from the list. That person is told, unless they set that channel's alerts to Nothing.

Photos, files and voice notes

Use the paperclip to add photos or files (up to 4 MB each), and the microphone to record a voice note. Links show a short preview.

Reactions, threads and more

  • React with an emoji instead of writing "thanks" or "agree".
  • Reply in thread keeps a side conversation together, on the right, without filling the channel. "3 replies" under a message opens it.
  • More holds Edit and Delete for your own messages, Copy link, and Report to moderators. Only Special Minds moderators see reports, and the person isn't told who reported them.
5 · Talking

Polls, and when a post waits

Polls

Write the question, press the chart button and add the answers. Choose whether people pick one or several, and when the poll closes. Everyone sees the results as they come in.

Why a post might wait, or not go

Each channel has its own rules, set by Special Minds. If one stops your post, the community tells you why.

You seeWhat it means
Waiting for a moderatorIn some channels, new members' posts (and in a few, every post) are checked before others see them. Usually within the day.
Held for reviewIt has a phone number, an email, or a word on the watch list. A moderator looks before it appears.
Slow modeOne post every so often, to keep a busy channel calm.
Posting hoursSome channels only take posts at certain times, such as 7 am to 11 pm. You can still read.
Only staff post hereAnnouncements, or a channel locked for a while. You can still react, and reply where allowed.
Children's privacyNever write a child's full name, school or family details in open channels: use initials and ages. Photos where a child's face can be seen are not allowed, unless a channel says otherwise.
6 · Questions

Forums and Ask a specialist

A forum keeps each question as its own topic with a title, so answers are easy to find months later.

  • New topic (top right): a clear title, the details, and photos or files if they help.
  • All, No replies yet, Mine filter the list. Topics without replies are a good place to help.
  • The person who asked, or a moderator, can mark the reply that helped most as the best answer. The topic then shows Answered.
6 · Questions

Ask a specialist, for families

In Families · UAE, parents ask questions and verified Special Minds specialists answer.

Asking without your name

Tick Post without my name and other members see "A parent". Special Minds staff can still see who asked, so they can help.

Not medical advice

Answers come from parents and specialists. For your child's care, speak to their own doctor or therapist.

Case talk, for specialists

Specialists ask each other for ideas in Case talk. Never name a child, a school or a family there. If a post has a word from the caution list, you're reminded before it goes.

7 · New cases

Opportunities, for specialists

When Special Minds has a new case, it's posted once in an Opportunities channel. The specialists who fit it are alerted, and say "I'm interested" with one tap.

  • I'm interested: add a short note if you like, such as when you're free. Only Special Minds staff see who is interested.
  • Not for me: tells Special Minds it doesn't suit you. You can change your mind.
  • Details: the full description.
  • Open, I'm interested, Filled at the top sort them.

Who is alerted, and what happens next

You're alerted when an opportunity asks for your role, in your city, in a language you speak (and, when the family asks, your gender). If Special Minds shortlists you, you're told "You're shortlisted" and they get in touch. When it's filled, everyone interested hears that too.

Keep your availability up to dateIn My account → Profile → Availability, switch I'm open to new cases on or off and say when you're free. Only Special Minds staff see this, and they look at it first.
8 · Coming up

Events and the library

Training days, webinars and parents' meetups live in Events channels. Guides and templates live in the Library.

  • Going, Maybe, Can't go: tell Special Minds whether to expect you. When places are limited, the count shows.
  • Online events: the joining link shows once you say you're going.
  • Add to calendar puts it in your phone or computer calendar.
  • Reminders: you're reminded a day before (Going or Maybe) and within the hour (Going).

The library

Search, or pick a category such as Visual supports or Data sheets. Download a file or Open a link. Special Minds adds to the library and keeps it tidy.

9 · For families

The families' space

Families · UAE is for parents and carers only, apart from the specialists' spaces. Special Minds moderators look after it.

Community feed

Share wins, questions and ideas with other parents, with photos and polls. Like and comment on others' posts.

  • Please don't share photos where a child's face can be seen.
  • For your first days, your posts are checked by a moderator before others see them.
  • Phone numbers and emails are held for review, to keep everyone safe.

Groups

Chat with parents who share your situation: Autism, ADHD, Speech delay, Dubai parents, Abu Dhabi parents and Arabic-speaking parents. Join them from Browse channels.

Ask Special Minds for help

Need a shadow teacher, therapy or an assessment? Press Ask Special Minds for help at the top of the channel list.

  1. Choose what you need.
  2. Add your child's age, city and a few words, if you like. No need for your child's full name.
  3. Check your name and number, and how you'd like to be contacted.

It goes to Special Minds only, not to the community. The team calls or messages you.

10 · One to one

Private messages

Messages are private conversations between two people, or a small group of up to eight.

  1. Open Messages and press New message, or press Message on someone's profile.
  2. Find the people by name and write your first message.
  3. Press Start the conversation.

Who can message whom

Specialists can message specialists, and parents can message parents. Messages between a parent and a specialist are off: agreements with families go through Special Minds. Special Minds can change this (section 23).

Something wrong?Use More → Report to moderators on any message. Special Minds can stop someone messaging, or remove them from the community.
11 · Staying in touch

Notifications

The bell holds everything meant for you: mentions, replies, new opportunities that fit you, announcements, event reminders and answers to your requests.

Choose what each channel tells you

Click the bell at the top of a channel and choose Everything, Mentions and replies, or Nothing. A busy chat can be set to mentions and replies, while Announcements and Opportunities tell you everything.

Pop-ups on your phone and computer

  1. Open My account → Notifications and press Turn on pop-ups. Allow notifications when the browser asks.
  2. Press Send a test to check.
  3. Do this once on each phone or computer you use.
iPhone and iPadFirst add the community to your Home Screen: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the Home Screen icon and turn pop-ups on there.
11 · Staying in touch

Email, WhatsApp and quiet hours

  • Email me announcements and opportunities I haven't read: a few hours after they're posted, only if you still haven't seen them.
  • A summary by email: none, every evening, or every Sunday.
  • WhatsApp: only for urgent news and opportunities that fit you.
  • Quiet hours: no pop-ups between the times you choose. Everything still waits in your bell.

Urgent messagesSpecial Minds can mark a broadcast urgent, for example when a centre closes. Urgent ones pop up even during quiet hours.

16 · Bringing people in

Inviting people

Admin → Invites makes links and QR codes: one shared link for a WhatsApp group or an event, or a personal link for one person.

  • Space and They join as: specialist, parent or carer, Special Minds team, or guest.
  • Also add them to private channels, such as Shadow teachers.

16 · Bringing people in

18 · The shape of it

Spaces and channels

Admin → Spaces and channels. A space is a separate community with its own members, channels and rules. Make a new one for another country or group.

  • Who it's for: specialists, families or everyone.
  • How people join: invite link only; ask to join, an admin approves; or anyone signed in can join.
  • Questions for people joining: one per line. End a line with * to make it required.
  • Community rules, in English and Arabic, shown to everyone joining.
  • Hold phone numbers and emails for review stops members swapping contact details without a moderator seeing it first.
  • Switched on: off hides the whole space.
18 · The shape of it

A channel's settings

Right now

Lock now and Staff only for now are quick switches for a heated moment. Archive puts a channel away.

Who sees it

Open: everyone in the space can find and join it. Private: listed, but only the people you add can read it. Hidden: only its members know it's there. Open channels can add everyone automatically.

What members may do

A grid of See, Post, Reply, React and Upload for specialists, parents and guests. Staff can always do everything.

When and how

  • Posting hours, on the space's own clock.
  • Slow mode: one post every 30 seconds to one an hour.
  • Posts wait for a moderator: never, from new members, or every post.
  • Locked or Staff only post.
  • Allow posting without a name (shown as "A parent").
  • Children's photos allowed: off asks people to confirm no child's face can be seen.
  • Case discussion rules: a reminder, and a check for caution words.
19 · New cases

Posting an opportunity

In an Opportunities channel, press New opportunity. It's posted once and only the specialists who fit are alerted. Their answers come back to you.

  • Role and city are needed. Add the area, start date, days and hours, the child's age and needs, and the languages.
  • Specialist: anyone, female or male, when the family asks.
  • Headline: left empty, it's made from the role and area.
  • Pay is seen by staff only.
  • Nudge on WhatsApp reminds matching specialists who haven't opened it after 2 hours.
  • Check who matches shows how many specialists fit before you post. Post it later picks a time.
  • Never put the child's name or the family's details in it.

Who is alerted

Active specialists in the space with that role, in that city, who speak one of the languages, and of the gender asked for. If the channel is private, only its members.

Profiles decide the matchA specialist with no city or role on their profile won't be alerted. Importing from Talent fills these in.

20 · Telling everyone

Broadcasts and scheduled posts

A broadcast is one message to a chosen group: in their bell, as a pop-up, and by email or WhatsApp if you choose. Use it for news that matters, such as a centre closing.

  • Who gets it: pick spaces and groups (specialists, parents, the Special Minds team), or narrow it by city, role or language. Leave a group empty for everyone.
  • How: always in the bell and as a pop-up; also by email; also on WhatsApp.
  • Urgent pops up even during people's quiet hours. Keep it for real emergencies.
  • Send later picks a time.
  • Check how many people it reaches before sending.
20 · Telling everyone

Scheduled posts and announcements

Staff see a calendar button, Post later, in the message box and on new events and library items. Admin → Scheduled posts lists everything waiting, with Post now and Cancel.

When they go outScheduled posts go out within a minute of their time while anyone has the community open. If nobody does, they go out as soon as someone opens it, and at the latest at the daily check at 6 pm UAE time.

Announcements

  • Post news in each space's Announcements channel. Everyone in the space is in it.
  • Pin to the top (More on the message) keeps an important post in view.
  • Staff see Seen by under each announcement.
  • People who haven't read an announcement get an email after a few hours, if they asked for it.
  • Close replies on a post when the conversation is done.
21 · Safety

21 · Safety

Blocked words

Add words and phrases in any language, and choose what happens:

  • Blocked the post can't be sent.
  • Held for review it waits in Moderation.
  • Caution in case talk a reminder in case discussion channels, and a flag for staff.

Phone numbers and emails are caught automatically in spaces that hold contact details.

When someone breaks the rules

  1. Remove the post with a short, kind reason.
  2. Pause their posting for a day or a week if it happens again (section 17).
  3. Suspend while you talk to them, or ban if they must not come back.
Reports are confidentialThe person reported is never told who reported them. Look at each report the same day: members are told a moderator will.

23 · The whole community

Delivery log, audit log and downloads

Delivery log lists every email and WhatsApp message the community sends: sign-in codes (the code itself is never kept), invites, digests, nudges and broadcasts. "Simulated" means it wasn't sent because that service isn't connected.

Audit log shows who did what and when: sign-ins, removals, bans, setting changes and downloads.

Downloads, at the bottom of Overview: members, opportunity answers, event RSVPs and help requests, as spreadsheets. Each download is written in the audit log, because they hold personal details.

24 · Going live

Before you invite everyone

The community is live at community.specialminds.app with its spaces and channels ready, and no members yet apart from the team. Do these first.

  • Connect email. Add the Resend key and a sending address on the hosting, so sign-in codes reach people. Until then nobody else can sign in.
  • Decide on WhatsApp. Codes and alerts on WhatsApp need a Meta WhatsApp Business account and approved message templates. Email alone is enough to start.
  • Check where families' data may be kept. The database is in Frankfurt. Confirm this is acceptable for UAE families before they join.
  • Plan for storage. Photos and files are kept in the database, and the free plan holds 0.5 GB. Upgrade before families share many photos.
  • Make your moderators and agree who looks at Moderation each day.
  • Read every space's rules and questions, and each channel's settings, in English and Arabic.
  • Try it on the preview as a specialist, a parent and an admin.

Your first week

  1. Day 1: import the specialists from Talent and send their invites. Post a welcome in Announcements.
  2. Days 2 to 5: post real opportunities, answer questions quickly, and verify the BCBAs and therapists.
  3. Day 6 or 7: open Families · UAE with a shared link or QR code. Approve requests the same day.
Help people turn on pop-upsMost of the value is in being told: new cases, replies, events. In your welcome post, ask everyone to turn pop-ups on (section 11) and iPhone users to add the community to their Home Screen first.
25 · Questions

Questions people ask

I didn't get my code.

Wait a minute and check your spam folder. Make sure you typed the email or number you joined with. Codes work for 10 minutes; ask for a new one if it ran out.

Is there an app to download?

No download is needed. Open community.specialminds.app and add it to your Home Screen (section 13).

Who can see what I post?

The members of that channel. Private channels are seen only by their members, and the families' space is separate from the specialists' spaces.

Can a parent message me privately?

Not unless Special Minds changes the setting. Agreements with families go through Special Minds.

Who sees that I'm interested in an opportunity?

Only Special Minds staff. Other specialists don't see your name.

Why is my post waiting for a moderator?

New members' first posts, posts with a phone number or email, and some channels are checked first. It usually appears within the day.

Why can't I post in this channel?

It may be an announcements channel, outside its posting hours, in slow mode, or locked for a while. You can still read and react.

How do I switch to Arabic?

My account → Language, or the العربية button on the sign-in page.

How do I stop pop-ups at night?

Turn on quiet hours in My account → Notifications. Urgent messages from Special Minds still come through.

I have a new phone number or email.

Ask a Special Minds admin to change it. It's how you sign in, so you can't change it yourself.

I lost my phone.

Sign in on another device and use Sign out everywhere else (My account → Sign-in and security).

How do I leave a channel?

Click the channel's name at the top and choose Leave channel, or use Browse channels.

Someone is being unkind.

Use More → Report to moderators on the message. They won't know it was you.

How do I delete my account?

My account → Your data. You can also download everything the community keeps about you.

For the team: a specialist isn't getting opportunity alerts.

Check their profile has the right role, city and languages, that they're in the space, and that they're active.

For the team: how do I remove someone?

Open them in Admin → Members: pause their posting, suspend or ban (section 17). Remove their posts from each post's More menu.

For the team: can we undo a ban?

Yes. Open the member and reinstate them. Their email and number can join again.

For the team: who do we ask about the hosting?

Email, WhatsApp, the CRM and Talent links, and a new owner are set up by Special Minds' developer, not in the app.
